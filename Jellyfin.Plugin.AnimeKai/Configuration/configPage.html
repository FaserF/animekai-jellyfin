Anime Kai & Fan-Edits Configuration

Universal anime fan-edits and direct HLS stream orchestrator for Jellyfin. Supports Re:Zero - The Perfect Run, One Pace, Naruto Kai, Bleach Kai, and self-hosted Consumet HLS Gateway with adaptive player switching, chapter metadata, and automated library synchronization. (GitHub: FaserF/animekai-jellyfin)

100% Direct TLS/HTTPS Client Streaming & Downloads: All streams (.strm) and downloads strictly use direct private HTTPS connections (Cloudflare CDN, Pixeldrain Direct API, Consumet HLS). Zero BitTorrent or P2P networks are ever used — zero public swarm IP exposure and zero upload/seeding legal concerns.
